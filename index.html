guitarauxiliator

This is a tool to automate music theory ideas for people whose brains hurt.


In an effort to maintain brevity and ease of reading quickly, I've written all ♯/♭ notes only the ♯ note. I know it's heresy, but it doesn't actually change anything and makes things easier.


You will notice a quirk with the circle of fifths info where you might see something like "D♯ ♭". Until I figure out how to get around this, this just means the ♯ and ♭ cancel each other out and you just consider it the regular chord.
